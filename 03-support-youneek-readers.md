# Support YouNeek YouNiverse readers

**Audience:** YouNeek Studios support team  
**Document type:** Customer-support procedure  
**Status:** Portfolio revision for product review · October 23, 2026

Use this guide to help readers subscribe, investigate access and reading problems, and route cancellation requests. It also covers missing newsletter downloads, which follow a separate delivery process. Each procedure ends with a resolution check or a handoff to the person who can investigate further.

## Before you reply

Read the full message and confirm that your reply will reach the reader. For requests forwarded from a contact form, check the sender and reply-to fields.

Use information the reader has already provided. Ask only for missing details needed to investigate: the account email address, the action they attempted, and what happened. Never ask for a password, a verification code, or full payment-card details. Ask readers to hide personal and payment information in screenshots.

An email address identifies the account to investigate; it does not by itself authorize account changes or disclosure of account details. Follow the team's approved verification process before either action.

## Help a new reader subscribe

Use this procedure for a reader who wants a new subscription. If they already subscribed or report a charge, investigate the existing account first; do not direct them to create another subscription.

1. Direct the reader to [YouNeek YouNiverse](https://app.youneekstudios.com/) and ask them to select **Subscribe**.
2. Ask them to review the offer and renewal terms displayed on that page before completing sign-up. Use the current offer; do not promise a trial length or price from an old email template.
3. Direct them to the **iPhone App** or **Android App** download link on the YouNeek YouNiverse home page.
4. Ask them to sign in to the app with the account they used to subscribe.

**Check the outcome:** Ask whether they can sign in and open a book. If either action fails, continue with the investigation procedure below.

Suggested reply:

> Hi [name],
>
> You can get started at https://app.youneekstudios.com/. Select **Subscribe**, review the current offer and renewal terms, and follow the sign-up instructions. Then use the iPhone or Android download link on the home page to install the app and sign in with the same account.
>
> If you get stuck, let me know which step you reached and what you see on the screen. Please don't send your password or payment details.

## Investigate an app problem

Start by identifying where the problem occurs. A sign-in failure, a book that will not open, and an unexpected charge need different information.

1. Confirm the email address used for the subscription. Do not assume it matches the address used to contact support.
2. Identify the affected device and whether the reader is using the website or a mobile app.
3. Collect the details for the reported symptom:

   | Symptom | Details to request |
   | --- | --- |
   | Sign-in failure | Exact error text, approximate time of the attempt, and device or browser. |
   | Book will not open or load | Book title, where loading stops, whether other books open, and app version or browser if available. |
   | Subscription access or payment problem | What the reader expected, what they see instead, when they subscribed or noticed the problem, and any non-sensitive confirmation or error text. |

4. Record any steps already attempted and their results. If the reader has not described them, ask before recommending repeated attempts.
5. Escalate unresolved access, reading, subscription, or payment problems to the app engineering team using the handoff below. Do not infer that a charge failed or a subscription is inactive from an access error alone.

Suggested reply when more information is needed:

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm sorry you're having trouble [signing in/opening the book/accessing your subscription]. To help us investigate, please send [only the missing details from the table above]. If there's an error message, the exact wording or a screenshot would help. Please hide any personal or payment information in the screenshot.
>
> We'll use those details to check what is happening.

### Prepare the engineering handoff

Use the team's approved internal support channel and current contact directory. Include the original request so the reader does not have to repeat it, and remove credentials or unnecessary personal information before forwarding.

```text
Issue: [short description]
Account email: [shared only in the approved internal channel]
Affected book or feature: [name, if applicable]
Environment: [website or mobile app; device; version if available]
Time observed: [date, approximate time, and time zone if known]
Expected result: [what the reader was trying to do]
Actual result: [what happened, including exact error text]
Steps already tried: [action and result]
Evidence: [sanitized screenshot or relevant confirmation text]
Requested action: [specific investigation or verification needed]
Customer update: [what support has told the reader]
```

If a detail is unavailable, mark it **not provided**. Do not fill gaps with assumptions or delay an otherwise useful handoff to complete every field.

## Handle a cancellation request

Treat cancellation, account deletion, and a refund as distinct requests. Do not use account deletion as a cancellation procedure or promise a refund.

1. Confirm the email address associated with the subscription.
2. The public subscription page lists **Subscription → Cancel Subscription**. Direct the reader to that route. If they cannot find or use it, pass the request to the app engineering team for assistance.
3. Ask the reader to confirm the result. A request to cancel is not proof that cancellation has completed.
4. If the reader has not confirmed the result within 24 hours of your cancellation reply, follow up. If there is still no response, ask the app engineering team to verify the subscription status.
5. Tell the reader the verified outcome. Include an access end date only if the system or an authorized team member has confirmed it.

**Check the outcome:** Record the cancellation confirmation or the pending engineering handoff. Keep the request open while the subscription status remains uncertain.

## Find a missing newsletter download

Use this procedure when a reader signed up for a free download delivered by email. If they are trying to open a book inside the app, use [Investigate an app problem](#investigate-an-app-problem) instead.

1. Confirm which download they requested and the email address they used to sign up.
2. Ask them to check their spam or junk folder for the delivery email.
3. If they find it, ask them to mark it as **not spam** and try the download link.
4. If the email is missing or the link fails, forward the details to the newsletter owner for follow-up. Include the download name, sign-up email, and what the reader has already checked.

**Check the outcome:** Confirm that the reader can access the download. Do not promise an immediate resend before the person handling delivery has confirmed the next step.

## Close the loop

Summarize the result in plain language: what was resolved, what the reader should do next, or who is investigating. Keep an unresolved issue open and carry forward the details already collected. When the reader or an authorized team member confirms the outcome, record that confirmation with the request.
